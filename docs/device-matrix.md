## Access ranking for an open algorithm project

**Best for an interoperable consumer data layer:** Apple HealthKit, and—with actual permissions confirmed—Xiaomi Health and Huawei Health Kit. **Best for developing signal-processing methods:** device-specific Polar SDK, EmotiBit or a contracted ODM that grants raw data. **Best to study consumer feedback logic:** WHOOP, Fitbit Air, Amazfit and Apple. These are project-fit assessments based on the documented interfaces, not rankings of medical accuracy.

Community-supported [Gadgetbridge](https://gadgetbridge.org/gadgets/) can help inspect supported Xiaomi, Zepp and Huawei models locally. It is a separate, unofficial route whose compatibility and pairing requirements may change; its existence does not make vendor firmware open source.

## Due-diligence questions for every vendor

Ask for exact fields, units, sampling cadence, waveform availability, quality indicators, retention while offline, firmware/API versioning, rate limits, regional availability, clinical validation by population and **written commercial rights** to store, analyze and distribute derived data. Request a sample export and run it through an adapter before making a product commitment.
